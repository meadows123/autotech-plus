Warning signs

Get your gearbox up and running

The first sign of gearbox trouble is often a jolt or a hesitation when the gears change. Catch it early and the repair is usually smaller and cheaper. Book a check if you notice any of these:

  • A burning smell
  • Oil leaking under the car
  • Clutch slip: revs rise but the car doesn’t pick up
  • Jerky, harsh or delayed gear changes
  • Judder when pulling away
  • Engine or gearbox warning light, or limp mode
