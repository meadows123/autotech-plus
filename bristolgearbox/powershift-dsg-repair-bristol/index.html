Powershift & DSG repair Bristol

Why dual-clutch gearboxes go wrong, what to look out for, and how we fix them.

Get a free quote Call 0117 329 2050

28 February 2021 · Updated 16 October 2026

Ford’s Powershift and the Volkswagen Group’s DSG are both dual-clutch gearboxes. They give you the convenience of an automatic with the efficiency of a manual, but they are more complex than either, and they need a specialist when something goes wrong. Here’s what you need to know.

How a dual-clutch gearbox works

A dual-clutch gearbox is effectively two gearboxes in one housing. One clutch handles the odd gears and the other the even gears, so the next gear is always pre-selected. A gear change is simply one clutch releasing as the other engages, which is why the changes are so quick and smooth. A control unit (on DSG, the mechatronic unit) decides when to change and operates the clutches.

Symptoms to look out for

  • Judder when pulling away or in slow traffic
  • Slipping, or a delay before the car moves
  • Jerky, harsh or delayed gear changes
  • A flashing gear display or warning message
  • Limp mode or loss of drive
  • A burning smell or oil leaks

Problems often start small. A slight judder in traffic can become a slipping clutch or a failed control unit if it’s ignored, so it pays to get it checked early.

Why diagnosis comes first

The same symptom can have several causes: worn clutches, a faulty control module, a dual-mass flywheel, contaminated clutches from a leaking seal, or simply a clutch that needs relearning. Guessing gets expensive. We use Bosch diagnostic equipment with dedicated gearbox interfaces to read what the gearbox controller has recorded, then give you a report and a quote before any work starts.

What a repair involves

Depending on the fault, we may renew the clutches, repair or replace the control unit or mechatronics, replace seals and actuators, rebuild the gearbox or fit a quality replacement unit. After clutch or controller work, the gearbox is recalibrated so it drives as it should. Every repair comes with a warranty.
