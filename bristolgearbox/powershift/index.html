What is a Powershift gearbox?

Powershift is Ford’s six-speed dual-clutch automatic gearbox. It works much like Volkswagen’s DSG: inside are effectively two gearboxes, each with its own clutch. One clutch handles the odd gears and the other the even gears, so the next gear is always ready. The result is quick, smooth changes and lower fuel consumption than a traditional automatic.

There are two main versions. Smaller Fords such as the Fiesta, Focus, B-Max and EcoSport use a dry-clutch Powershift, where the clutches are operated electrically. Larger models such as the Mondeo, S-Max, Galaxy and Kuga use a wet-clutch version that runs in oil.

Common Powershift problems

  • Judder or shudder when pulling away, especially in slow traffic
  • Slipping, or a delay before the car moves
  • Harsh, clunky or jerky gear changes
  • Loss of drive or dropping into neutral
  • “Transmission malfunction” or overheating warnings
  • Oil from leaking seals contaminating the dry clutches

Powershift diagnostics

We use up-to-date Bosch diagnostic systems with dedicated interfaces for automatic gearboxes. They read everything the gearbox controller has recorded, so we can pinpoint the fault precisely rather than guess. You get a report on the gearbox’s condition, including things like the oil level, and every repair is based on it.

Powershift repair and replacement

Depending on the diagnosis, we can renew the clutches, repair or replace the control module (TCM), replace seals and actuators, rebuild the gearbox or fit a high-quality replacement unit. We assess each component, so parts that work aren’t replaced unnecessarily.

Powershift servicing

Because of its dual-clutch design, a Powershift is more involved to service than a conventional gearbox. After clutch or control-module work, the clutches need a relearn with diagnostic equipment so the car drives as it should. We do this as standard.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Powershift repair cost?

It depends which part needs work: the dual-mass flywheel, the clutch, the gearbox itself or the control module, or a combination of these. Once we’ve diagnosed the fault we give you a clear quote before any work starts. Call us for a guide price.

Is there a warranty on Powershift repairs?

Yes. Our repairs and rebuilds of Powershift clutches and gearboxes come with a warranty, and we’ll confirm the terms with your quote.

How long will a repaired Powershift gearbox last?

A professionally repaired and rebuilt clutch or gearbox is restored to the same specification as a new part. With the same driving style and use, you can expect a service life similar to the original components.

Does a rebuild change how the gearbox drives?

No. A rebuilt Powershift clutch, gearbox or controller is restored to factory specification, and its technical parameters don’t change.
