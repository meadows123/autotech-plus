What is Powershift?

Powershift is the automatic gearbox in many Fords, including the Fiesta, Focus, Kuga and Mondeo. It changes gear quickly and smoothly, but it’s more complicated than a normal automatic, so it needs someone who knows it well.

Signs something’s wrong

  • Juddering when pulling away
  • Slipping, or a delay before the car moves
  • Jerky or clunky gear changes
  • A gearbox warning on the dashboard

What we do

We plug into your car’s computer and test the gearbox to find exactly what’s wrong. Then we explain it and give you a price. Often it’s the clutch or the part that controls the gearbox, not the whole thing, so we only replace what needs replacing. Every repair comes with a warranty.
