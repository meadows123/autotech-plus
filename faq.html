MOT & booking

How long does an MOT take?

Usually around 45 minutes to an hour. You’re welcome to wait, or drop your car off and we’ll let you know when it’s ready.

How early can I get my MOT done?

You can have your MOT up to a month (minus a day) before your current certificate runs out and still keep the same renewal date. You can check your due date on GOV.UK.

What happens if my car fails its MOT?

We’ll explain exactly what failed and why, and give you a clear quote for the repairs. No work goes ahead without your OK.

Servicing & maintenance

How often should I service my car?

We recommend servicing your car every 10,000–15,000 miles or once a year, whichever comes first. Regular checks help us spot potential issues before they become expensive.

Which fluids should I regularly check and top up?

The key fluids are engine oil, coolant, brake fluid and power steering fluid. Always follow the manufacturer’s guidelines for the right levels and types.

How often should I replace my tyres?

It depends on your driving style, road conditions and tyre type. Check your tyres every 6 months or every 10,000–15,000 miles. They need replacing when the tread depth falls below the legal limit.

Do I need to use original replacement parts?

Not always. There are high-quality aftermarket parts that are just as good as the originals. The important thing is a trusted supplier, and we’ll advise on the right parts for your car.

What are the most common causes of breakdowns?

Battery issues, worn brake pads, electrical problems, engine overheating, tyre damage and fuel system issues. Regular servicing and fixing small issues promptly helps prevent bigger breakdowns.

Warning signs & repairs

What are the signs of worn brake pads?

Squeaking noises, a spongy brake pedal, longer braking distances or the steering wheel vibrating when you brake. If you notice any of these, get your brakes checked straight away.

What causes steering wheel vibration while driving?

Unbalanced wheels, damaged tyres, worn suspension components or a steering system issue. We can pinpoint the exact cause and fix it.

What causes fluid leaking under the car?

It could be oil, coolant, brake fluid or power steering fluid. If you spot a leak, bring it in so we can find the cause and repair it.

What are the symptoms of exhaust problems?

Loud noises, engine misfires, higher fuel consumption, loss of power or exhaust leaks. If you notice any of these, book in for diagnostics.

Performance

What are the benefits of engine remapping?

More power, torque and throttle response, improved fuel efficiency and better overall performance. We tailor the remap to your car and keep engine parameters safe.
